SURE Walk Texting Laptop

Is this your first time using this texting laptop?

This laptop runs the automated texting server and iPhone Bluetooth bridge for tonight's rides. We can walk you through the 3 quick steps to get ready, or you can jump straight in.

Step 1 of 3

📱 iPhone Bluetooth Connection

Make sure the SURE Walk iPhone has Bluetooth turned on and is nearby.

Checking bridge…
Windows Phone Link runs in the background. The phone pill in the top bar turns green when connected.
Step 2 of 3

📊 Excel Add-in (Rides Sync)

Open tonight's rides workbook in Excel and click the SURE Walk Texts add-in.

Direct Laptop Connection
The add-in goes straight to Auto-text and connects to this laptop at 127.0.0.1:8787. No GroupMe setup needed — all texts stay on this laptop!
Step 3 of 3

✨ You're Ready to Text!

When dispatch logs new rides in Excel, riders receive automated confirmation texts.

  • Riders reply YES to confirm or CANCEL to cancel.
  • Statuses update live in the Excel sheet and in this dashboard.
  • You can view conversation feeds, send custom replies, or add walk-in rides anytime.

You can reopen this walkthrough anytime by clicking ❓ First time? in the top bar.
